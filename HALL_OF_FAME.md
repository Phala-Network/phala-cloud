# Phala Cloud Security Hall of Fame

We thank researchers whose responsible reports lead to meaningful, verified
security improvements in Phala Cloud.

## Recognition policy

- Phala may recognize a verified security improvement even when it receives no
  severity rating or does not qualify for a bounty. Recognition and financial
  rewards are separate decisions, each subject to Phala's review and approval.
- Only the earliest substantive report identifying the same underlying root
  cause is considered for recognition and bounty review. Priority is determined
  from the received submission and supporting evidence sufficient to identify
  and validate the issue.
- Later reports of that root cause, including variants requiring the same
  remediation, are duplicates and receive no separate Hall of Fame recognition
  or reward. Substantively distinct root causes are reviewed separately.
- Public attribution requires the reporter's explicit prior opt-in consent to
  the name or handle and contribution summary. Publication also requires Phala's
  approval that the issue is resolved and the details are safe to disclose.
  Participation in reporting alone does not grant publication consent.
- Entries contain only an approved name or handle, a brief safe description of
  the contribution, and an optional public fix or advisory link. Private contact
  information, report contents, and internal review decisions stay confidential.

The [security policy](SECURITY.md) governs reporting, product scope, researcher
conduct, and bounty eligibility. A Hall of Fame entry does not assign a severity
rating or guarantee a financial reward.
